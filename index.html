Welcome, Wretch

You stand at the gates of the Crypt with naught but your wits, your wits, and a deck. Each room of the dungeon is laid out as four cards. Survive every room, exhaust the deck — and the Crypt is yours.

From the main menu, choose your road: Adventure (full descent — knight, lanterns, the door swings as you pass) or Quick Play (cards only, fast taps for a quick round). Both modes share the same rules.

The Black Suits — Foes

♠ Spades and ♣ Clubs are monsters. Their value is their menace: number cards by face, Jack = 11, Queen = 12, King = 13, Ace = 14.

Strike a monster bare-handed and suffer damage equal to its value. With a weapon, damage is reduced — but the weapon remembers.

The Red Suits — Boons

♥ Hearts are healing draughts. Quaff one to restore HP equal to its value — but you cannot exceed your maximum vitality of 20.

♦ Diamonds are weapons. Equip one and reduce monster damage by its value (minimum 0).

The red royals only return at easier difficulties — see the next page.

The Five Difficulties

The Crypt offers five tiers from Damned (default, harshest) down to Wanderer. Each step easier eases ONE mechanic AND adds back one rank of red royals to the deck.

Damned — 3 flees, no flee mid-room, no undo, no peek, deck 44.
Vanquisher — adds flee mid-room, +red Jacks (deck 46).
Adventurer — adds undo, +red Queens (deck 48).
Apprentice — adds peek-next-room, +red Kings (deck 50).
Wanderer — unlimited flees, full deck of 52.

The Weapon's Curse

A blade dulls upon iron flesh. After your weapon strikes a monster, the next monster you fight with that weapon must be of a strictly lower value. Strike a 10, then no foe of 10+ may face that blade again.

You may always set the weapon aside and meet a foe bare-handed (Shift-click on desktop). You may always replace your weapon by equipping a new diamond.

Rooms & Fleeing

Each room shows four cards. Resolve them in any order. After three are resolved, the lone surviving card carries forward and three new cards join it.

You may flee a room as your difficulty allows. On Damned, fleeing is only legal if NO card has yet been resolved — once you commit to a card, you commit to the room. On easier tiers, you may flee mid-room.

Fleeing returns the unresolved cards to the bottom of the deck, then four new cards rise.

The Crypt — Adventure Mode

In Adventure, the cards become physical encounters: a slime, a mimic, a healing draught on a pedestal, a sword embedded in stone. Click any one and the knight walks to it, fights / drinks / picks it up, then walks back.

Clear all four and the knight crosses the room toward the iron door at the back. The door swings open, the camera follows you through, and a deeper chamber forms — each stage of the descent (Antechamber → Catacombs → Reliquary → Sanctum → Throne) wears its own decay.

Empty the deck and the door opens onto the treasure room.

Vitality & the D20

Your soul is bound to a great D20 die in the chamber's right wall. Twenty pips of vigor. When the pips reach zero, the Crypt claims you.

Watch the die — it spins when you bleed and pulses when you heal. Listen for its hum.
