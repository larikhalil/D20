Welcome, Wretch

You stand at the gates of the Crypt with naught but your wits and a deck of forty-four enchanted cards. Each room of the dungeon is laid out as four cards. Survive every room, exhaust the deck — and the Crypt is yours.

The Black Suits — Foes

♠ Spades and ♣ Clubs are monsters. Their value is their menace: number cards by face, Jack = 11, Queen = 12, King = 13, Ace = 14.

Strike a monster bare-handed and suffer damage equal to its value. With a weapon, damage is reduced — but the weapon remembers.

The Red Suits — Boons

♥ Hearts are healing draughts. Quaff one to restore HP equal to its value — but you cannot exceed your maximum vitality of 20.

♦ Diamonds are weapons. Equip one and reduce monster damage by its value (minimum 0).

The Crypt has banished the red royals — only numbered hearts (2–10) and numbered diamonds (2–10) remain.

The Weapon's Curse

A blade dulls upon iron flesh. After your weapon strikes a monster, the next monster you fight with that weapon must be of a strictly lower value. Strike a 10, then no foe of 10+ may face that blade again.

You may always set the weapon aside and meet a foe bare-handed. You may always replace your weapon by equipping a new diamond.

Rooms & Fleeing

Each room shows four cards. Resolve them in any order. After three are resolved, the lone surviving card carries forward and three new cards join it.

You may flee a room — but only as your difficulty allows. Fleeing returns the four cards to the bottom of the deck, then four new cards rise to greet you.

Vitality & the D20

Your soul is bound to a great D20 die in the chamber's right wall. Twenty pips of vigor. When the pips reach zero, the Crypt claims you.

Watch the die — it spins when you bleed and pulses when you heal. Listen for its hum.
